IonWatch

Space Technology · AI/ML · Research

What it is

An ionospheric scintillation monitoring system designed to provide insights into space weather disturbances. It grew into an incubated startup.

How it works

A low-cost station, a NavIC/GNSS receiver on a Raspberry Pi, watches satellite signals as they pass through the ionosphere. It computes the S4 and phase scintillation indices, the standard measures of how much a signal flickers, through Solar Cycle 25. An offline pipeline turns recorded sessions into per-minute indices, fade events and plots.

Result
IDE Bootcamp 2026 (AICTE & MoE’s Innovation Cell): Winner, Best Performing Team and Pitch Excellence Award
Built with
NavIC/GNSS receiver, Raspberry Pi, Python

GNSS Guardian

Autonomous Systems · Computer Vision · IoT

What it is

A GPS-denied drone navigation system that uses optical flow and visual terrain mapping to navigate when satellite signals become unreliable.

How it works

A Raspberry Pi 4 edge module watches the health of every satellite signal: per-satellite signal-strength baselines, fix quality and sudden jumps in position, with a state machine that avoids false alarms. When it detects jamming or spoofing, it falls back to a downward camera to guide the drone home. Optical-flow odometry runs at 30 fps on the Pi, and a camera-versus-GNSS cross-check flags slow spoofs. On hardware, it detected jamming in about two seconds.

Result
SGU HackX 1.0 (SDGI Global University, Oct 2026): Second Position among 70 teams, ₹11,000 cash prize
Built with
Raspberry Pi 4, Quectel L86 GNSS, Pi Camera, Python; Shi–Tomasi and Lucas–Kanade tracking, ORB keyframes
Tested on
A handheld rig carrying the module

Trishul Net

Earth Sciences · Edge AI · IoT

What it is

A low-cost early-warning network for flash floods and debris flows in the side valleys of Uttarakhand.

How it works

Cheap sensor nodes on stable ground beside a stream sample ground vibration at 200 Hz, filter it to 5–45 Hz, and run a trigger and a small on-device classifier. A gateway checks that at least two nodes agree, that the upstream node fired first and that the speed is plausible, which rules out earthquakes. It then estimates the arrival time and sounds the village siren locally, with no internet needed. The models are trained and tested on real public debris-flow recordings from Illgraben, Switzerland.

Status
Research prototype, not a certified warning system
Built with
ESP32-S3 sensor nodes and a gateway, Python

Lunar South-Pole Ice Detection

Space Technology · Geospatial · Research

What it is

Water-ice detection and mission planning for crater F2 near the lunar south pole, built for the Bharatiya Antariksh Hackathon 2026.

How it works

A five-stage Python pipeline runs over about 50 GB of NASA LOLA topography (10 m per pixel) and ISRO Chandrayaan-2 DFSAR polarimetric radar. It maps permanently shadowed regions, models illumination, detects ice, picks a landing site and plans a rover traverse. Ice is inferred from the circular polarization ratio, after showing that backscatter inversion can’t tell lunar ice from regolith, and every stage checks itself against published ground truth.

Findings
Landing Site A at −87.87°S, 82.66°E: 29.3% annual illumination, 5.1° mean slope, 14.6 km from F2. A planned rover traverse of 15.7 km.
Built with
Python, rasterio, GDAL, NumPy, SciPy

Sand Grain Mapping System

Computer Vision · Edge AI · Earth Sciences

What it is

A low-cost camera-based system for sand grain mapping and analysis, developed for the Smart India Hackathon 2025.

How it works

A Raspberry Pi 5 with a Pi Camera photographs a sample, separates each grain with image processing, and measures the grains with statistical analysis. The whole system sits in a 3D-printed casing.

Result
Smart India Hackathon 2025: Top 5 Finalist nationwide, Ministry of Earth Sciences
Built with
Raspberry Pi 5, Pi Camera, 3D-printed casing

Gravity

AI · Systems · Personal Tools

What it is

A personal assistant system that remembers my working day. It records what I do, sorts what I tell it into tasks, notes and client records, and writes me a morning brief.

How it works

A Python system captures git, browser, window and clipboard activity into a portable SQLite brain, and turns natural-language Telegram messages into tasks, notes and client records. Code syncs across my two machines with encryption, while the research trail stays local, behind a firewall that strips out secrets. Search runs over full text and local embeddings, with local Whisper for speech and language models routed under a budget.

Built with
Python, SQLite, Telegram Bot API, local Whisper, NVIDIA, Gemini and Groq models

Perihelion: The Last Trajectory

Web Game · 3D · Sci-Fi

What it is

A cinematic space-survival game, built for a game jam on the theme LOOP.

How it works

You fly the research spacecraft Kestrel through three hostile sectors, Kestrel Drift, Halcyon Shear and the Shard Belt, managing fuel, hull and engines as you weave through asteroid fields. Lost transmissions piece together what happened to the missions before you, and the signal you follow keeps repeating.

Status
Playable prototype, in the browser
Built with
HTML5, in 3D; graphics made with AI assistance
